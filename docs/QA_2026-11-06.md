# QA and clinical UI review — 6 November 2026

This review used the built local application at `http://127.0.0.1:4173` with a disposable, in-memory database and synthetic answer, retrieval, and email services. The synthetic answers test software behavior only. They do not establish clinical accuracy or live provider reliability.

## Prioritized findings

| Priority | Finding | Status and evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | An untouched PERC form displayed “PE ruled out” and said no testing was indicated. | Fixed. Each of eight findings now starts as Not assessed, and the form produces no count or prompt until all are recorded. The completed record never claims to rule out PE. Verified in component, built-browser, and in-app browser tests. |
| High | Untouched HEART, qSOFA, and Centor forms displayed scores and risk or treatment conclusions from assumed inputs. | Fixed for these four tools. Inputs now start unassessed; generated prompts, copies, and UI wording avoid individual discharge, sepsis exclusion, or antibiotic decisions. Verified in component and built-browser tests; qSOFA was inspected in the in-app browser. |
| Medium | The 22-tool tab strip hid later tools and did not show the selected tab after navigation. | Fixed. A labeled direct picker lists every tool, and the active tab scrolls into view. Verified with a component regression and in-app browser inspection. |
| Critical | Calculator interpretations, medication cautions, retrieved claims, and generated answers lack independent clinical validation. | Open. The product remains educational. A qualified clinical owner needs to review intended populations, exclusions, wording, and a held-out evaluation set before patient-facing use. |
| High | Other calculators still show demonstration values or unchecked criteria and some display population risk percentages. | Open. Audit each tool for unassessed inputs, population-specific validity, and wording that could be mistaken for an individual prediction. Avoid releasing these as clinical decision support pending review. |
| High | Live PubMed, cloud/local model providers, Google OAuth, and production operations were not exercised in this fixture session. | Open. Verify with deployment credentials and approved test data, then complete the release gates in `CURRENT_CAPABILITIES_AND_RELEASE_GATES.md`. |
| Low | The fixture-backed chat can answer while Account reports no configured provider, because the test runner injects a synthetic generator without a provider key. | Open in local QA only; production provider readiness must be checked separately. |

The PERC correction follows the [ACEP clinical policy](https://www.acep.org/siteassets/new-pdfs/clinical-policies/clinical.policy.suspected.acute.venous.thromboembolic.disease.pdf), which limits the rule to appropriately assessed low-risk emergency patients. The qSOFA wording follows [current Surviving Sepsis Campaign guidance](https://www.sccm.org/survivingsepsiscampaign/guidelines-and-resources/surviving-sepsis-campaign-adult-guidelines) against using it as a sole sepsis screening tool. The Centor wording avoids treatment from symptoms alone, consistent with [CDC strep throat guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/group-a-strep/hcp/clinical-guidance/strep-throat.html).
